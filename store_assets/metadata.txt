## Full Description (Max 4000 chars)
Watts is a modern, lightweight utility designed for users who want to understand their device's power consumption and charging performance in detail.

Built with the latest Jetpack Compose and Material 3 standards, Watts provides a beautiful, fluid interface that changes color based on your energy state.

Key Features:
- Real-time Wattage: See exactly how many Watts your device is using or receiving.
- Charging Intelligence: Automatically detects charging speeds (Slow, Medium, Fast, Super Fast) with visual indicators.
- Live Metrics: Monitor Current (mA), Voltage (V), and Battery Temperature (°C) in real-time.
- Minimalist Design: A clean, ad-free dashboard focused on the data you need.
- Battery Health: Keep an eye on your battery capacity and status.

Whether you're testing a new charger, a different cable, or just curious about your device's energy footprint, Watts gives you the transparency you need.
